W. A. Jones For BAA — Feb 2026

This isn't exactly what's
in your brief.
But it's close. And it's real.

This work already exists. It's what comes naturally — candid, observational, people moving through the world without knowing or caring they're being photographed.

Nobody ever asks for this. They should.

Moving through the city
Transit — environmental context
Unguarded moments
On the brief
What matches
Candid posture. No acknowledgment of camera. Environmental context. Motion and presence. Imperfection. The feeling of being somewhere, not performing somewhere.
What's honest
These are existing shots — not BAA product. The dedicated shoot delivers the brief as written: airports, hotel entrances, walking sequences, transition moments. This is the sensibility that shoot comes from.
The approach
Small camera. No assistant. No lighting. The grain and pulled blacks are intentional — not a filter applied after, a decision made before.
Why this matters for BAA
Agency paparazzi shots look like agency paparazzi shots. This looks like someone actually saw something worth photographing. That's a different conversation for your brand.

Ready to shoot the brief properly. Let's talk about what that looks like.

W. A. Jones  ·  New York City
alex@wajonesphotography.com
